DAY 1 ACTIVITIES

🌅 Morning – Street Dance and Field Demonstration

The morning activities will feature the Street Dance and Field Demonstration, where every department will showcase their dance performance. Each department will present their creativity, energy, teamwork, and school spirit through their performances.

🎯 Afternoon – Laro ng Lahi

In the afternoon, the celebration will continue with Laro ng Lahi. Every team will participate in different traditional Filipino games, promoting teamwork, camaraderie, sportsmanship, and fun among the participants.
